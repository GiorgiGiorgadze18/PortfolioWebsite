WHO AM I

From IT to design.
From design to development.

I’m Giorgi Giorgadze, a Digital Designer & Creative Developer based in Tbilisi, Georgia. I work across UI/UX, visual design and interactive web experiences.

My background in IT support taught me to investigate problems, understand systems and communicate practical solutions. Design brings that thinking closer to the people using a product. Development helps me turn an interface into something they can explore and use.

I work from structure to detail: clarify the problem, map the experience, prototype the interface and refine it through testing. My current interests include design systems, responsive interfaces, creative coding and 3D web experiences.

Education and certifications

Ilia State University — Bachelor of Sciences and Arts Faculty, 2022–2026. Design training at Foster Academy and Skillwill, with team leadership and conflict management certificates from DataArt.
