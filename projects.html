Tsero App

Mobile App · UI/UX · 2026

The name “Tsero” comes from the Georgian word for stork, symbolizing delivery, movement and speed. The app explores modern, user-centered food ordering with browsing, courier tracking, checkout, payment, and profile settings. Branding, logo, color system, UI/UX and interactive prototyping were designed by Giorgi Giorgadze. This is a personal portfolio concept.
