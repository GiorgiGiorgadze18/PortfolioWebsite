Inside the composition

A playful vector world

A 2025 independent poster study created in Adobe Illustrator, with a cheerful character and a party-inspired palette.

The character comes first

The Earth character leads the composition. Its original silhouette, expression and colours stay intact as it rises above the poster.

A grounding gesture

The oval shadow sits below the character, giving the illustrated pose a sense of weight. Its hidden section is reconstructed for this presentation.

A frame for the message

The green gradient panel stays beneath the character and its shadow. It groups the original event text, date and device-switch-off message.

Colour with a playful purpose

The owner identifies #AD94D9, #A26FBA and #7BBA5B. Purple sets the atmosphere; the green gradient helps the bold text stand out. The owner names The Bold Font.

Back to the original

The layers return to the untouched original image. This is a limited-angle 2.5D study; hidden panel and shadow regions are reconstructed, not recovered vector source.
